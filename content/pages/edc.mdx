### bags
i have three bags i switch between.

for lighter days, i use a Lucy and Yak Dara, which i have in a lovely starry
pattern. it can't hold much so i don't really bring it out as it's hard to fit
my journal in it.

most days i use a radley crossbody as it fits everything i need in a day,
including my water bottle, which is so convenient.

if i'm carrying my laptop, i'll use the GOTBAG x Field Notes roll-top backpack.

### writing
i carry both kokuyo and leuchtterm1917 notebooks in A5, dotted.

the L1817 is my bullet journal, and the kokuyo is for just jotting down stuff,
like lists etc.

as for pens, i always have my LAMY al-star in sage green with me,
it's a beautiful writer and i love it so much. i also keep a uni jetstream on me,
in case i need to use a ballpoint for anything.

### reading

i try to keep a book on me at all times, either physical, or through an e-reader.

my current e-readers are an Xteink X4, which is lovely but i bricked it,
so it's currently out of action :(
i also have a (jailbroken) kindle paperwhite 11th gen which is nice,
but _ew amazon_.

### ephemera
