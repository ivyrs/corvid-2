This site is built using [Astro](https://astro.build) and hosted on [Love Computer](https://lovecomputer.net).

It's styled with [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com), because I like how quick it is to make CSS do what I want.

The colorscheme is [uchu](https://uchu.style).

The fonts are IBM Plex Sans and Berkeley Mono. Icons are provided by Font Awesome.

### Domains

I chose ivy.rs for three reasons

- short, easy to say - i-vee dot r s
- my name is IVY RoSe
- i like the rust programming language, even if i am terrible at it

ivy.rs is bought through [istanco.net](https://istanco.net) as Porkbun doesnt support .rs domains (yet?)
All of my other domains are bought through [Porkbun](https://porkbun.com), or [internetbs.net](https://internetbs.net).

You can also hit this site through the following domains: ivyro.se, ivyrose.gay, ivyrose.mom
